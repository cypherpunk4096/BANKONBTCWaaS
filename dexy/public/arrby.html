02 · Cross-chain monitor — backend-fed, multi-chain leaderboard

This connects to the ARRBY backend (backend/) over WebSocket. The backend runs one signal scanner per configured chain concurrently and ranks them together — this shows you which chain has the best same-chain flash-loan opportunity right now, so execution stays a single atomic transaction on whichever chain wins. It is not an atomic cross-chain trade — bridging isn't instant, so a genuine buy-here-sell-there-across-chains trade carries settlement risk this monitor doesn't take on. 06 · Autoearn & treasury — unattended earning with guardrails

Autoearn runs the whole detect→execute loop for you, but it can't invent profit: every execution still passes the contract's on-chain minProfit check, so a bad iteration just reverts (gas spent, principal safe). The value it adds is unattended operation with hard rails — a per-run notional cap, a cooldown, a consecutive-failure circuit breaker, optional compounding, and periodic treasury sweeps. For real headless operation use integrations/autoearn.js; this panel mirrors its controls and its live state.
