BANKON ALGO Wallet as a Service · non-custodial · twin of BANKON BTC

Your 25-word recovery phrase and key are generated here, in your browser (algosdk). BANKON never receives them — only your public address (watch-only) and already-signed transactions reach the node. Tandem with BANKON BTC; this twin talks to your Algorand node.

checking Algorand node…

1 · Create a wallet (client-side keygen)
2 · Track an address (watch-only)
3 · Send (build → sign locally → broadcast)

Ports: this ALGO WaaS runs on :4444 (twin of BANKON BTC WaaS :8088). Non-custodial invariant identical — the server rejects any request containing a phrase/secret key.
