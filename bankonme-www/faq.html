What is your business plan?

To distribute an open-source, value-added operating-system platform on USB pendrive that is suitable for banking with cryptocurrency. A full 51% of net profits will be distributed amongst the projects that make bankonmeOS possible.

Why is your webpage so ugly?

A fair question…. I will try to make it easier to read. In the future people that join will have a choice of color schemes as I start to remember how to write CSS or I find a suitable plugin.

2026 update. Answered at last — the WordPress chrome, trackers, the news feed and the ticker widget are gone; this is one clean, self-contained page with a readable dark theme and the bitcoin-orange brand. No external calls, nothing to load, nothing to track.

Why are you using WordPress?

WordPress is open source and is a very scalable web platform. I also enjoy the Joomla and the Silverstripe CMS. Maybe something interesting will happen here one day.

2026 update. Something did — this page is now plain static HTML/CSS you can read, fork, and host anywhere, in keeping with the sovereignty the project is about.
